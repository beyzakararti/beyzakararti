## About

I'm an Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at the University of Duisburg-Essen in Duisburg, Germany. I like projects where hardware, software and research meet. Most of my repositories are private; I'm happy to give access on request.

## What I do / interests

- **LLM development** — the AI assistant on my personal website, built on the Claude API, and a social media automation panel with FastAPI and AI-generated content.
- **Web design & deployment** — websites and web apps with HTML/CSS/JavaScript, TypeScript, React and Next.js, deployed on Vercel and Cloudflare.
- **Circuit design** — power electronics and PCB design, for example a modular 330 W power station with a battery management system and thermal and battery-life simulations.
- **3D design** — interactive 3D with Three.js, such as the gesture-controlled [Gesture 3D Viewer](https://github.com/beyzakararti/gesture-3d-viewer) and a 3D globe for route simulation, plus CAD work in SolidWorks, Catia and Siemens NX.
- **R&D / research** — signal processing and machine learning for engine-sound fault detection, blockchain-based energy trading, and other papers listed on my [research page](https://www.beyzakararti.com/research.html).

**Languages:** English (C1), German (A2–B1), Turkish

## Projects

- **[Gesture 3D Viewer](https://github.com/beyzakararti/gesture-3d-viewer)** — control 3D models and slides with hand gestures in front of a webcam.
- **[Pati-Nabız](https://app.vetaisoft.com)** — pet health and care management platform (live; private repository).
- **FRAGWATT** — a portable, modular 330 W power station for gaming laptops (private).
- **ATLAS** — a 3D global traffic observatory with route simulation (private).
- **MotorSense** — a mobile app that analyses engine sounds to spot early faults (private).
- **DablSync** — website and apps for a granule dosing system by Sabyeza Dynamics (private).
- **Arkoveya Energy Forward** — a pilot platform for corporate renewable-energy forward PPAs (private).
- **Inkasa Foods social media panel** — one dashboard to manage a brand's social accounts (private).
- **[beyzakararti.com](https://www.beyzakararti.com)** — my personal website, including the AI assistant byeza.ai.
